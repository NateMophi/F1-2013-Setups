F1 2013 is a video game developed by Codemasters based on the 2013 Formula One World Championship. It uses the EGO Engine.[4] F1 2013 was released in Europe in 2013 on PlayStation 3, Microsoft Windows, and Xbox 360 on October 4. A digital version was released worldwide on the Steam Store, as well as on 8 October for the PlayStation Store and 15 October for Xbox Games on Demand, with a further release on Mac OS X by Feral Interactive in March 2014.[3] The game was taken down from the stores due to probable license expiration in 2017, whilst the online servers were shut down on 21 March 2024.[5][6]
